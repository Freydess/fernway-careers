Hiring on Fernway

    Questions, answered

    Does it cost anything?

    No. Making a profile, applying and posting jobs are all free.

    How does Fernway decide how well a job matches me?

    It compares your profile with the job: whether it’s in the area you want, whether it suits your experience level, and how many of the job’s skills you have. Each job shows the reasons, so you can judge for yourself.

    Who can see my profile?

    Only the employers you apply to. Nobody can browse or search for job seekers.

    Do I need a resume?

    It helps, but it’s optional. Add a link (Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive) set to “anyone with the link”, or just your portfolio, GitHub or LinkedIn.

    Is an AI deciding who gets hired?

    No. Fern only helps you fill in your profile. Employers read each application and make every decision themselves.
