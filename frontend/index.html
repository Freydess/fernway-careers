        FAQ

        Questions, answered

        Do I need a resume?

        It helps, but it’s optional. Share a link (Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive), or just your portfolio, GitHub or LinkedIn.

        Is a bot deciding whether I get hired?

        No. Fern only collects your answers and suggests roles. A person on our hiring team reads every application and makes every decision.

        What’s the difference between the guided chat and the AI chat?

        The guided chat asks a fixed set of quick questions with buttons. The AI chat lets you answer in your own words and ask about Fernway. Both end with a summary you can edit before sending.

        How long until I hear back?

        We review new applications every week and reply to everyone by email within 7 days.

        What happens to my information?

        We only use it to review your application. The privacy notice explains what we store, where, and how to ask us to delete it.
