Privacy notice

What we store

  • Your account: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Job seekers: the profile you fill in, such as skills, experience, location, phone number, pay expectations and links to your resume or portfolio.
  • Employers: your company details and the jobs you post.
  • Applications: a copy of your profile at the moment you applied, your note to the employer, and each status change, such as when it was seen, accepted or rejected.
  • Notifications about those applications.

Please don’t add sensitive details such as ID or passport numbers, your date of birth, religion, health or bank details. Employers don’t need them to review an application.

Who can see it

  • An employer sees your profile only when you apply to one of their jobs, and only for that application.
  • Nobody can browse or search for job seekers.
  • Anyone can see open jobs and the employer’s company details.
  • The Fernway team can see accounts and applications to run the site and help with problems.

Services we use

Fernway runs on a few outside services, which store or process data for us:

  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Render runs our server, and Neon hosts the database.
  • OpenRouter passes your messages to an AI model when you build your profile by chatting with Fern. Only the chat is sent, not your whole account.
  • HubSpot and Notion help our team keep track of applications.
  • Typeform runs our short interest form for people without an account. Zapier copies its answers into Notion, and emails employers about new applications through Gmail.

About Fern, the AI assistant

Fern only helps you fill in your profile. It never applies for you and never decides anything about an application: employers read applications and decide themselves. You check and save your profile yourself after the chat.

How long we keep it, and your choices

  • You can change your profile at any time. Applications you already sent keep the copy that was sent.
  • You can withdraw an application at any time from your Applications page.
  • To see, correct or delete your account and everything in it, email hello@fernway.example. We reply within 30 days.
  • We keep your account until you ask us to delete it. This is a university project, so the whole site and its data will be deleted when the project ends.

Demo mode

When the site shows “Demo mode” at the bottom, nothing is sent to our server: accounts, jobs and applications are saved only in your own browser. “Reset demo data” removes them.
