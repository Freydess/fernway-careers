Privacy notice

How we handle your application

What we collect

Only what you choose to tell us when you apply:

  • Your name, email, and (optionally) phone number and time zone
  • The area and kind of role you’re interested in, your experience level and years
  • Links you share, such as a portfolio, GitHub, LinkedIn or resume link
  • When you could start and, if you tell us, your pay expectations
  • Your answers in the chat, and a short summary of them for our hiring team

We don’t ask for ID numbers, your date of birth, or other sensitive details. Please don’t include them.

Why we collect it

To review your application, contact you about it, and consider you for roles that fit. We only process your application after you tick the consent box, and the record notes when you agreed.

Where it’s stored and who sees it

  • Our application database, which only our hiring team can access
  • Our recruiting tools: a HubSpot contact record and a card on our Notion hiring board
  • If you use the AI chat, your messages are sent to our AI provider (Groq) to write Fern’s replies
  • If you apply through our Typeform, Typeform processes your answers first

Links you share stay links: we don’t download or copy your files.

How long we keep it

Up to 12 months after your last application, so we can consider you for future roles, and then we delete it from our database and tools. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, email careers@fernway.example. We’ll reply within 30 days.
